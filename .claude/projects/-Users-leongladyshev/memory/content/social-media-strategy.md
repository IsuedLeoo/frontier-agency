**Target Audience:** Miami small business owners, entrepreneurs, founders
**Brand Voice:** Professional but approachable, data-driven, local Miami focus

## 1. Posting Schedule

### LinkedIn
| Day | Time (EST) | Content Type |
|-----|-----------|--------------|
| Tuesday | 8:00 AM | Educational / data-driven post |
| Thursday | 12:00 PM | Case study / story-driven post |
| Friday | 9:00 AM | Quick tip / engagement post |

**Frequency:** 3x per week
**Rationale:** Tuesday morning catches professionals starting their week. Thursday midday catches lunch-break scrolling. Friday morning catches end-of-week planning mode.

### Instagram
| Day | Time (EST) | Content Type |
|-----|-----------|--------------|
| Monday | 7:00 PM | Carousel (educational) |
| Wednesday | 6:30 PM | Reel or single image (behind-the-scenes / tip) |
| Saturday | 10:00 AM | Story series (engagement / poll) |

**Frequency:** 3x per week (feed posts) + daily Stories when possible
**Rationale:** Evening posts perform best for small business owners (they're off work and scrolling). Saturday morning catches owners doing weekly business planning.

## 2. Content Themes by Platform

### LinkedIn Themes
1. **Data & Insights** — Industry statistics, cost analyses, ROI numbers that make business owners think
2. **Pain Point Education** — Highlight problems Miami small businesses face (missed calls, admin overload, lead leakage)
3. **Behind the Build** — Show the process of building custom AI solutions (establishes expertise)
4. **Client Results** — Case studies, testimonials, time-saved metrics (once available)
5. **Miami Business Local** — Tie into local events, weather, culture ("It's hurricane season — is your business covered?")

### Instagram Themes
1. **Carousel Education** — Bite-sized tips, "Did you know?" stats, list-style value
2. **Visual Before/After** — Show the transformation automation creates
3. **Brand Personality** — Miami lifestyle meets business growth (beach + business aesthetic)
4. **Quick Tips Reels** — 15-30 second actionable tips for business owners
5. **Engagement Posts** — Polls, "this or that," questions in captions

## 4. Engagement Tactics

### Daily (when accounts are active)
- Respond to every comment within 2 hours during business hours
- Reply to DMs within 4 hours
- Like and comment on 10 posts from Miami business owners daily
- Engage with posts using relevant hashtags (3-5 comments/day)

### Weekly
- Share one client success story or testimonial (once available)
- Post one Story with a poll or question sticker
- Engage with local Miami business accounts (network building)
- Share/repost relevant local business content with commentary

### Monthly
- Review analytics (reach, engagement rate, follower growth)
- A/B test posting times
- Refresh hashtag sets based on performance
- Plan next month's content calendar

### Growth Tactics
- Partner with Miami business accounts for cross-promotion
- Use LinkedIn articles for long-form SEO content
- Encourage clients to tag the agency in their posts
- Run occasional "tag a business owner who needs this" engagement posts
