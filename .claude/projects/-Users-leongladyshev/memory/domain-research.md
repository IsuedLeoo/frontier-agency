## Top 3 Recommendations

### 1. frontier.agency (PRIMARY RECOMMENDATION)

**Why**: The .agency TLD is the most brand-perfect match. It reads as "frontier.agency" — clean, modern, and immediately communicates what the business is. The TLD itself IS the business type. It's available and affordable (~$3.63-24/yr).

**Pros**:
- Perfect semantic match: [brand].[business type]
- Short and memorable (no "agency" repeated in the name)
- Modern TLD that signals tech-forward positioning
- Very affordable, especially at Cloudflare (~$24/yr at cost)
- Available NOW

**Cons**:
- Less familiar than .com to some users
- Some users may instinctively type .com
- Email addresses would be @frontier.agency (slightly unusual)

**Mitigation**: Can redirect a .com variant to this as backup.

### 2. frontieragency.ai (SECONDARY RECOMMENDATION)

**Why**: As an AI automation agency, the .ai TLD directly signals the core service offering. It's the industry-standard TLD for AI companies in 2026. It's available.

**Pros**:
- Perfect signal for AI-focused business
- Industry-standard for AI companies (credible in the space)
- Available NOW
- Strong for SEO in AI-related searches

**Cons**:
- Expensive (~$80-94/yr, ~$115/yr renewal at Namecheap)
- Requires 2-year minimum registration
- Less familiar to non-tech audiences
- Higher long-term cost

**Best for**: If positioning as a premium AI-first brand.

### 3. gofrontieragency.com (SAFETY RECOMMENDATION)

**Why**: The .com extension is still the gold standard for credibility, especially for local Miami businesses who may not trust newer TLDs. "Go" prefix adds a call-to-action feel. It's available and cheap.

**Pros**:
- .com = maximum trust and familiarity
- Affordable (~$10-12/yr)
- "Go" prefix is energetic and action-oriented
- Easy to communicate verbally ("go frontier agency dot com")
- Available NOW

**Cons**:
- Longer than other options
- Less distinctive/brandable
- "Go" prefix can feel generic (gogle, goproless, etc.)

**Best for**: If the priority is maximum credibility with non-tech local businesses.

## Domain Naming Trends for AI Agencies (2026)

Based on research:
- **.ai domains** are the standard for AI-first companies but have seen significant price increases in 2026 (Namecheap raised to ~$93/yr). Minimum 2-year registration required.
- **.agency domains** are gaining traction for creative/digital agencies. Affordable and semantically meaningful.
- **.io domains** remain popular for tech startups but are expensive (~$45-50/yr at Cloudflare) and frontieragency.io is taken.
- **.co domains** are the closest .com alternative but frontieragency.com is taken.
- **Hyphenated domains** (e.g., frontier-agency.com) are generally discouraged — harder to communicate verbally, look spammy.
- **Geo-specific domains** (e.g., frontieragencyfl.com) are good for local SEO but limit expansion.
- **"Go" prefix domains** are common for startups but can feel generic.
