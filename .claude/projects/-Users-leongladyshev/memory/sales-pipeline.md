# Sales Pipeline — Frontier Agency

## Status Key
- `NEW` — Just identified, not yet contacted
- `CONTACTED` — Called or emailed
- `INTERESTED` — Expressed interest, needs follow-up
- `NOT_INTERESTED` — Declined, moved to nurture
- `CALLBACK_REQUESTED` — Asked us to call back later
- `MEETING_SCHEDULED` — Discovery call booked
- `WON` — Signed a contract
- `LOST` — Chose not to proceed
- `NURTURE` — Long-term follow-up cadence

## Our Positioning
**We are an AI Agency, not a single-product company.** We consult with each business to understand their operations, then design and build custom AI automation solutions. This can include:
- AI receptionists and phone automation
- Sales automation and lead follow-up systems
- Customer service chatbots and support automation
- Marketing automation (email, social, content)
- Workflow and operations automation
- Data entry and processing automation
- Inventory and supply chain automation
- Custom AI agents for any business process

Every engagement starts with a discovery call to understand what would have the biggest impact for that specific business.
